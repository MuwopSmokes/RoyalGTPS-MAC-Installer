Royal GTPS Setup
macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon and Intel

1. Unzip Royal GTPS Setup.zip and double-click Royal GTPS Setup.app.
2. If macOS blocks the first launch, and you trust the source of your ZIP,
   go to Apple menu > System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down
   to Security, click Open Anyway for Royal GTPS Setup, then confirm Open.
   An administrator may need to authenticate.
3. Close Growtopia. Click Install Royal GTPS, review the private CA prompt, and
   click Continue. Approve each macOS administrator prompt. The app
   tells you which step it is on. When setup finishes, click Launch Growtopia.
4. To restore your previous setup, close Growtopia and click
   Return to Original Growtopia.
   Approve the macOS prompts and wait for Previous settings restored.

If Install is disabled, your copy is awaiting a confirmed certificate
fingerprint. Ask the Royal GTPS distributor for an enabled build.
